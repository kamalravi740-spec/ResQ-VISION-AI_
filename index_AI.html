Helpful information

Help & resources

Simple guidance for using the platform and finding trusted assistance.

How to report an issue

Choose a category, describe what happened, add a location, and submit. Keep your reference number.

Immediate danger

If someone is in immediate danger, contact the appropriate local emergency service directly. This demo does not dispatch help.

Privacy reminder

Share only information needed for the report. Avoid posting sensitive personal details or another person’s private information.

Connection and drafts

This prototype saves submitted reports in browser storage. It is not a secure server, offline sync system, or permanent record.

Accessibility

Use keyboard navigation, readable text, and reduced-motion settings supported by your device.

Language access

A production version should provide reviewed translations and accessible language options.

People helping people

Volunteer with us

Learn about contributing to community support in a coordinated, safe way.

Make a meaningful contribution

Volunteers can help share verified information, support community outreach, and assist with coordination through an approved organization.

Before volunteering

  • Follow guidance from recognized local coordinators.
  • Do not enter hazardous areas or attempt tasks beyond your training.
  • Protect the privacy and dignity of people requesting help.
  • Use verified information and report concerns through official channels.

Volunteer values

Respect

Listen, preserve dignity, and support people without judgment.

Safety first

Follow approved procedures and local coordinator instructions.

Reliability

Share accurate updates and communicate clearly.
